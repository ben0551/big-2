      How to play

      • Goal: get rid of all your cards. The last player holding cards is the Shithead 💩
      • You have 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up on top, and a hand. Before play starts you can swap hand ↔ face-up cards (tap one, then the other).
      • First player: whoever holds a 3 with the lowest hand total. Nobody has a 3? Lowest single card.
      • Play a card equal or higher than the pile. Play several of the same rank together. Can't play? Pick up the pile.
      • Draw back up to 3 while the deck lasts. Hand empty → play face-up cards → then face-down cards blind.
      • 2 resets · 10 burns the pile and you go again · 7 next player must play 7 or lower · four of a kind on top burns.
      • Tap a card to select, tap it again (or Play) to play it.
